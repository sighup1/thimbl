Support

Thimbl

For help with Thimbl, email gsowards100@gmail.com. Include your device model and the app's version, which is shown at the bottom of Settings.

How many projects are free?

One project is free, with every feature. A yearly plan or a lifetime purchase lets you keep as many as you like. The example project added from Settings does not count.

What happens if my plan ends?

Every project you have already made stays open and editable. Only adding new projects beyond the free one needs a plan.

I bought a plan but the app says Free

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same account you used to buy the plan.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

In the app, open Settings, then Manage subscription. You can also cancel from your account's subscription settings on your device. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next year.

Where is my data kept?

Only on your device. It is included in your device's backups, and is deleted if you delete the app. See the privacy policy.

A size chart was read incorrectly

Every chart is shown for checking before it is added, and any value can be corrected afterwards on the chart's page. A photograph of just the chart, taken straight on and in good light, reads best.
