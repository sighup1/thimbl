Privacy Policy

Thimbl · Effective 25 November 2026

Thimbl is a sewing journal and fitting tool. It is designed so that what you record stays with you: the app does not collect, store or share any personal information on any server.

What the app stores

Everything you enter is stored only on your device: projects, patterns and size charts, measurement profiles, fitting worksheets, work logs, notes and the photographs you choose to add. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

If your device is backed up, this data is included in that backup in the same way as other apps' data. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Photographs and documents

When you add a photograph or a PDF, you choose it yourself, and the app receives only the items you pick. Size charts are read from them on the device. They are not uploaded.

Purchases

Plans are bought through the App Store. Payment details are handled by the store and are never seen by the app or by us. The app is told only whether a plan is active, so that it can allow more projects.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require an account.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not share data with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
